Grounded in `PRODUCT.md`, `DESIGN.md`, and the real screens under

## Positioning

Most competing couple apps fall into two camps: bare D-day counters
("Been Together", "My Love Anniversary Tracker" — a date, a widget, a push
notification, nothing else) or do-everything chat apps ("Coupled" —
messaging, calls, shared to-do lists, with the day counter buried as one tab
among many). InLove sits deliberately between them: a focused *relationship
control center* built on four real pillars the app actually ships —
accurate day/anniversary tracking with smart staged reminders, curated gift
suggestions tied to real occasions and budgets, a cloud-synced shared memory
album, and genuine real-time 1-to-1 partner pairing ("Set Love" — not just
typing in a date alone, but a paired account kept in sync on both phones).
Fully bilingual EN/VI from day one, not a bolted-on translation. The
differentiator in copy: "everything a couple actually needs, nothing you
have to manage" — not a chat replacement, not a bare counter.

Comparable apps checked (via web search, positioning reference only — no
wording copied): **Been Together – Couple D-Day Counter** (bare counter +
widgets + passcode), **Coupled – Relationship Tracker** (day counter bundled
into a full chat/calendar/call app), **My Love Anniversary Tracker / Love
Days** (counter + milestone reminders + widget, single-device).

# 2. Screenshot & Demo Video Shot List

General rule for every screenshot: use a realistic, non-round day count
(e.g. **247 days**, not `0` or `100`), real-looking (but fictional) partner
names, at least one real-looking photo in the memory album, and at least one
gift marked as a favorite — so nothing reads as an empty first-run state.

## 2.1 Screenshots

### 1. Home / Day Counter (`LoveHomeScreen`)
- **State**: Day counter showing a specific, non-round number (e.g. `247`
  days), both partner names and avatars filled in, ages and zodiac signs
  visible, a love quote visible, at least one milestone badge earned.
- **Caption EN**: "247 days and counting — right on your home screen."
- **Caption VI**: "247 ngày yêu và vẫn đang đếm — ngay trên màn hình chính."

### 2. Calendar & Reminders (`CalendarScreen`)
- **State**: Week or month view with at least one upcoming milestone shown
  with a countdown (e.g. "12 days to your 1-year anniversary"), plus one
  past anniversary and one birthday entry visible, not an empty calendar.
- **Caption EN**: "Never miss an anniversary — smart reminders, every time."
- **Caption VI**: "Không bỏ lỡ ngày kỷ niệm nào — nhắc nhở thông minh mọi lúc."

### 3. Memories Album (`MemoriesGridScreen`)
- **State**: Grid populated with at least 6–8 photos (mixed favorites and
  non-favorites, at least one marked favorite with the heart filled), not
  the empty-state card.
- **Caption EN**: "Every memory, saved and synced for both of you."
- **Caption VI**: "Mọi khoảnh khắc được lưu giữ và đồng bộ cho cả hai."

### 4. Gift Suggestions (`GiftScreen`)
- **State**: Gift ideas list scrolled to show 3–4 varied cards with real
  (not default/picsum) imagery once the cleanup in Section 3 is done, one
  item favorited, one item on the checklist marked done, a budget filter
  visibly applied.
- **Caption EN**: "Gift ideas that actually fit the occasion."
- **Caption VI**: "Gợi ý quà tặng phù hợp từng dịp lễ."

### 5. Settings / Pairing (`SettingsScreen` + `PairingScreen`)
- **State**: Settings screen showing an active "Paired" status with a real
  partner name (not "Guest"), language toggle visible in both EN and VI
  (two separate screenshots if the store allows, or one screenshot per
  locale's listing), PIN-lock option visible.
- **Caption EN**: "Paired in real time with the person you love."
- **Caption VI**: "Ghép đôi thời gian thực cùng người bạn yêu."

## 2.2 Demo / Preview Video (~20 seconds, 6 shots)

1. **0:00–0:03** — App opens on Home: day counter animates/counts up to
   `247`, floating hearts micro-animation plays.
2. **0:03–0:07** — Cut to Calendar: scroll reveals an upcoming milestone
   countdown and a milestone badge unlocking.
3. **0:07–0:11** — Cut to Memories grid: tap a photo to open it full-screen,
   tap the heart to favorite it.
4. **0:11–0:15** — Cut to Gift screen: scroll gift ideas, tap a heart to
   favorite one, check an item off the gift checklist.
5. **0:15–0:19** — Cut to Settings: show the "Paired" partner card, then
   tap the language toggle to switch EN ↔ VI live.
6. **0:19–0:22** — End card: InLove logo + tagline ("Đếm ngày yêu, giữ trọn
   kỷ niệm." / "Count your days, keep every memory.").
